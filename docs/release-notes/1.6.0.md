## New
- **YouTube and YouTube Music in the browser.** NotchHub can show and control YouTube tabs in Chrome, Arc, Brave and Edge. This is off by default; turn on "Detect YouTube in the Browser" in Settings › Media. It needs "Allow JavaScript from Apple Events" in the browser.
- **Synced lyrics** from LRCLIB, fetched only when you open them.
- **More media controls:** skip ±10 s, playback speed and Picture in Picture for YouTube, a like button, per-player volume, shuffle and repeat, and an "Up next" row for YouTube Music.
- **Island HUD.** The volume and brightness HUD is now a floating capsule under the notch. The bar takes the album art colour, you can drag or scroll it, and it shows AirPods battery. The volume key step can be set to 2, 5 or 10 %.
- **Refreshed alerts** with a colour wash, glow and a glowing countdown. Reduce Motion is respected.

## Fixes
- Brightness keys no longer get stuck at 75 %.
- Brightness never drops below the lowest step, and at 0 the backlight turns off completely.
- Browser track changes show up right away instead of after a few seconds.
- Track titles are no longer written to the system log.
