## New
- Assign a shortcut to show or hide the notch: Settings › Shortcuts › Show/Hide Notch.
- Media controls now live on the right of the notch and appear only when you point at them, so the status icons keep their room the rest of the time.

## Improvements
- Redesigned collapsed media: the artwork glows in its own color while playing and dims with a pause badge when paused; play/pause is a disc tinted to match the cover.
- Notifications from the app you already have in front no longer show up in the notch.
- The meeting mic button is now a simple icon: green while live, orange when muted.
