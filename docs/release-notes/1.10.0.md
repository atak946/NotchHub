## New
- Menu bar box: collect menu bar icons in a box that drops down from a single arrow. Click or right-click an icon there, and its window or menu opens below the arrow in most apps. Choose what to hide by ⌘-dragging icons across the divider. Off by default; turn it on in Settings › Notch & Appearance. Needs Accessibility access.
- Icons in the box can look as they do in the menu bar, such as CPU and RAM values or a VPN badge. Optional, needs Screen Recording access; macOS may show its recording indicator while the box is open.
- Quiet mode: mute notifications for an hour, until tomorrow or until you turn them back on, from the gear menu. In Settings › Alerts it can also start by itself in meetings or during Pomodoro focus (both off by default). Messages that arrive meanwhile are kept and summarized afterwards; security and battery alerts are always shown.

## Known limits
- A few apps need their icon back in the menu bar for a moment before they open.
- Icons that only react to a real right click may open their normal window from the box instead.
- The menu bar box relies on behavior Apple does not document, so a macOS update may affect it.
