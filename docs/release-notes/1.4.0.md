## New
- **Setup wizard** on first launch, with live permission status.
- **Diagnostics report** with personal details removed, plus a prefilled GitHub issue.
- **Volume and brightness HUD** in the notch.
- **Camera mirror** before meetings. The camera turns on only when you click.
- **System-wide mic mute** with ⌥⌘M, and a meeting indicator in the notch.
- **Headphone battery** for AirPods and Bluetooth headsets when they connect.
- **Clipboard history** in the shelf. Copies from password managers are skipped.
- **Shortcuts actions** for the timer, notes, profiles, alerts, modules and the mic.

## Improvements
- NotchHub wakes the system about 3× less often while idle (from about 171 to about 56 times a minute).
