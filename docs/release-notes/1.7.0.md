## New
- Choose how long notch alerts stay on screen: Settings › Notch Alerts › Time on screen (Short, Normal, Long).
- The "+N" pill beside the closed notch opens the Status tab when you hover over or click it.
- While nothing is playing, status icons that do not fit on the right of the notch move to the empty left side.

## Improvements
- Alerts no longer linger: hovering holds an alert for at most 30 seconds, a burst of messages from the same chat ends after 20 seconds, and alerts are shorter while others are waiting.
