## New
- **English.** NotchHub is now available in English and Turkish, with a language setting in the app.
- **Redesigned Settings** with clear categories, a sidebar and search.
- **Per-app notification privacy.** You can hide message content for chosen apps, for example WhatsApp but not Zoom. The sender and app icon stay visible, and **Show** reveals the content in place.
- **Charging animation** when you plug in the power cable.
- **Privacy indicator** when an app uses the microphone or camera.
- **Music visualizer** while something is playing.
- **Weather** in the notch, using your approximate location.
- **Quick calculator** in Notes.

## Fixes
- The menu bar menu no longer stutters while you move the pointer over it.
