## New
- The app menu (Settings, Pause, Mirror, Quit and more) moved into the notch: click the gear at the top right of the open panel. The menu bar icon now only appears while the notch cannot be opened, for example when NotchHub is paused.
- Opening NotchHub again from Spotlight or Finder shows Settings.
- The closed notch shows the name of the playing track beside its artwork. Point at the media controls to see the full title and artist.

## Improvements
- Track change alerts only appear when the next track starts by itself, not when you change it.
- The panel header adapts to wide notches: the weekday and the high / low temperatures give way when space is tight.
